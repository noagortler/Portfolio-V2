About

I'm a junior front-end developer and recent Circuit Stream bootcamp graduate. I build responsive web applications with React, JavaScript, TypeScript, HTML, and CSS, and I care about interfaces that are clear, practical, and easy to use. Before writing code, I think through how people will actually use each screen.

Before bootcamp, I spent over ten years in hospitality, including front-of-house leadership and training roles. That experience taught me to communicate clearly, support my team, and stay calm under pressure, and I bring those same habits to every project and team I work with.

When I'm not coding, I like to spend time with my cats, enjoy the outdoors (bonus points if it's sunny), and try out new ways to exercise.
